PathFinder avatars

Twelve original personas, each drawn as inline SVG from simple geometric shapes. Nothing is loaded from disk or the network, so there are no image files, no photographs and no licensed artwork anywhere in the app.

Why this replaced the old set. The previous twelve avatars were ten anime illustrations and two photographs of real children — a copyright and image-rights risk. These are generated by assets/js/avatar.js at runtime: a two-colour gradient plus one abstract motif per persona. Each persona has an English and a Swahili name.

The 3 Pillars of Kenyan Student Financing

1

Universities Fund (UF) Scholarship

Direct government scholarship determined through the Means Testing Instrument (MTI) calculated by KUCCPS based on public records.

  • Coverage: 30% to 70% of tuition
  • Eligibility: KUCCPS Public University Placement
  • Direct to Registrar: No cash to student

ALERT TRIGGER: Immediate SMS triggers upon KUCCPS placement release.

2

HELB Tuition Loans

Concessional revolving student loans disbursed directly to university accounts, repayable after graduation through employer payroll deductions.

  • Interest Rate: 4% Flat (Simple Interest)
  • Guarantor: 2 Kenyan guarantors (ID + KRA PIN)
  • Repayment: Starts 1 year after completion

ALERT TRIGGER: Automated M-Pesa disbursement tracking.

3

HELB Upkeep/Living Loan

Essential living stipend disbursed directly to the student's registered mobile money wallet to cover accommodation, meals, and books.

  • Annual Amount: KES 45,000 – KES 62,000
  • Disbursement: 2 equal semester tranches
  • Channel: Direct M-Pesa to student wallet

ALERT TRIGGER: Immediate SMS triggers upon tranche release.

Means Testing Instrument (MTI)

HEF Funding Categorization: Bands 1 through 5

Automated scoring factors include family income level, orphanhood status, county marginalization index, and disability status.
Placed in the wrong band? You have the statutory right to launch a free re-categorization Appeal within 30 days of initial publication on the HEF portal by uploading supplementary proof (death certificates, medical records).

Standard Operating Procedure

How to Apply: Clear Walkthrough

First-Time Undergraduates & TVETs
  1. Visit KUCCPS Placement Letter: Obtain your official KUCCPS admission letter with the student portal login credentials.
  2. Create Account on hef.co.ke: Use your valid KCSE Index Number, national ID number, and active mobile number.
  3. NEMIS / UPI & CUE Verification: System queries the Integrated Population Registration System (IPRS) and Ministry of Education NEMIS registry.
  4. Upload Guardian Documents: Clear Guardian's National ID, death certificates (if applicable), and household income declarations.
  5. Input Upkeep Payout Channel: Register your mobile money number matching your own national ID.
  6. Mandatory Financial Literacy Quiz & Consent: Complete the 2019 loan consent guidelines and acknowledge the repayment agreement.

Average First-Time Completion: 45 Minutes · 100% Paperless

Continuing Students (Subsequent Loans)
  1. Annual Subsequent Application: Complete online questionnaires in years 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6. It does not require re-uploading core identity documents.
  2. Channel 1: Official HEF Mobile App: Download from Google Play Store (App Name: HEF Kenya).
  3. Channel 2: Offline USSD *642#: Dial *642# on any Safaricom/Airtel line to check status without internet.
  4. Timelines: August & September: Subsequent loan windows open in August and close mid-September every academic year.
Automated SMS & Email Dispatch: Never miss a disbursement or appeal deadline

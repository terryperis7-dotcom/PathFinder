Terms of use

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Guidance, not placement

PathFinder estimates cluster points and cut-offs from public sources to help you plan. Official cluster points, cut-offs, placement and funding decisions come from KUCCPS, HEF and HELB. Always confirm anything consequential on their portals before you act on it.

Free to use

PathFinder is free for students. We never charge a fee, never take a commission, and will never ask for payment to create a KUCCPS or HELB account. Treat any such request as fraud.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. In local demo mode accounts live on this device only and are not recoverable if you clear your browser data. Do not create an account for someone else without their consent.

Tutors

Tutors are shown with a verification state per check; a badge appears only after a reviewer records that check. Rates are fixed per pilot band and no payment is taken inside PathFinder. Contact with a learner outside the platform is not permitted and the chat filter enforces it.

Acceptable use

Do not scrape the reference data, attempt to access another learner's record, or use PathFinder to harass anyone. Accounts can be suspended for abuse, and reports are reviewed before anything else.

No warranty

The service is provided as-is during a pilot. Data may be corrected or reorganised, and indicative figures change every intake. Nothing here is legal, financial or academic advice from KUCCPS, HEF, HELB or the Ministry of Education.

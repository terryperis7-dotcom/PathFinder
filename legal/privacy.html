Privacy Policy

PathFinder Kenya ("we", "us") respects your privacy and processes personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 and regulations issued by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

1. What we collect

Account details (name, email, phone, county, school, stage), academic results you enter, quiz answers, and usage data needed to run the service. We never sell personal data. Demo accounts contain clearly-labelled mock data only.

2. How we use it

To calculate your KUCCPS pathways, track progress, match you with institutions, scholarships and tutors, and to keep your account secure. We do not use your data for advertising or broker commissions.

3. Where it is stored

On your device (local cache for speed) and, when configured, on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row-Level Security. Access is limited to authorised staff under confidentiality obligations.

4. Your rights (DPA 2019)

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@pathfinder.ke. We respond within 72 hours. You may also lodge a complaint with the ODPC, Britam Tower, Upper Hill, Nairobi.

5. Children

PathFinder is designed for learners aged 13+. Guardian consent is required for users under 18 in tutor sessions, in line with the Children Act, 2022.

6. Cookies & local storage

We use local storage only to keep you signed in and remember your preferences (language, sidebar). No third-party tracking cookies.

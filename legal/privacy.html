Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019

Who we are

PathFinder Kenya is a private education-guidance pilot. It is not an official KUCCPS, HEF, HELB or TVETA service. In local demo mode everything you enter stays in this browser: no request leaves your device.

What we hold

  • Your name, email and county (and school if you add it).
  • Your subject grades, targets, logged assessments and quiz answers.
  • Your shortlist, and any tutor sessions or messages you start.
  • Nothing else. No location tracking, no contacts, no advertising identifiers.

Why we hold it

To compute your cluster points and pathway matches, to show your progress over time, and to let a tutor see the request you sent them. We never sell data, and we do not share it with brokers or agents.

Learner safety

Tutor chat is filtered: phone numbers, social handles and abusive language are blocked before sending, and every blocked attempt is logged for review. Any learner can report a tutor, and an administrator can suspend that tutor immediately.

Children

PathFinder is built for secondary-school learners. Where a learner is under 18, a guardian may request a copy of the record or its deletion, and should be the one to consent to tutor sessions.

Your rights

Under sections 26–40 of the Act you may access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to processing. Profile → Export my data downloads everything we hold as JSON, and clearing your browser storage removes it from this device in demo mode.

Retention and contact

Assessment logs are kept while your account is active and deleted on request. A Data Protection Officer will be appointed and registered with the ODPC before any production launch. Until then, write to privacy@pathfinder.ke .

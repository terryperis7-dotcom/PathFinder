Government funding · Higher Education Financing (HEF)

Funding & HELB Scholarship Hub

Understand means testing, avoid broker traps, and never miss a deadline.

✓ REAL DATA Sources: Universities Act (2012), HELB Act, HEF portal (hef.co.ke). Bands & amounts per official HEF framework.

1

Universities Fund (UF)

Government scholarship via Means Testing (MTI). Covers 30–70% of tuition, paid directly to your university.

2

HELB Tuition Loan

Concessional loan at 4% flat interest, paid to your university. Repay after graduation via payroll.

3

HELB Upkeep Loan

Living stipend (KES 45,000–62,000/yr) sent directly to your M-Pesa for accommodation, meals and books.

Placed in the wrong band? Appeal free on the HEF portal with supporting proof.

How to apply — first-time students

  1. Get your KUCCPS placement letter & portal login.
  2. Create your account on hef.co.ke with KCSE index + national ID.
  3. System verifies you via IPRS & NEMIS automatically.
  4. Upload guardian ID, death certificates (if any), income proof.
  5. Register your M-Pesa number for upkeep payments.
  6. Complete the financial literacy quiz & loan consent.

Average completion: 45 minutes · 100% paperless · USSD fallback: *642#

Cluster Points Calculator

Work out the exact weighted cluster points KUCCPS would use for your chosen course — and see which subject to push to reach the cut-off. Your results stay on your device.

THE FORMULA
w = √ ( ( r / 48 ) × ( t / 84 ) ) × 48
r = your points in the 4 cluster subjects (max 48)  ·  t = your points in the best 7 subjects (max 84)
Grade points: A=12 · A−=11 · B+=10 · B=9 · B−=8 · C+=7 · C=6 · C−=5 · D+=4 · D=3 · D−=2 · E=1

1 · Enter your KCSE subject grades

Leave a subject blank if you did not take it. Only subjects with a grade are counted.

2 · Choose the course cluster

Each KUCCPS cluster requires four specific subjects. We automatically pick your best valid combination — a subject can only be counted once.

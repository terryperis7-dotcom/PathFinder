About PathFinder Kenya

One trusted guide from classroom to career.

Choosing subjects, understanding KUCCPS cluster points, applying for HELB, and avoiding broker fees is confusing — especially on a small phone with slow internet. PathFinder puts official Kenyan education information in plain language, personalised to your marks and interests.

We are a private pilot compliant with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019. Your data is never sold, there are no ads, and guardians can view progress safely.

What PathFinder does

Matches you to pathways

A 60-second quiz + your scores → ranked degree & TVET options.

Tracks your marks

See subject trajectories against real KUCCPS cut-offs.

Explains funding

Universities Fund bands, HELB loans, scholarships, deadlines.

Lists real institutions

Verified universities, polytechnics & KMTC with cut-offs and fees.

Connects vetted tutors

TSC-verified teachers, safeguarding-checked, with learner-safe chat.
